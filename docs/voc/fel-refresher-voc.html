Front End Loader Refresher VOC

An assessment of an experienced operator's current skill on a wheeled front end loader. It is not initial training. The candidate brings evidence of prior training and experience; the assessor verifies theory, checks and an uninterrupted practical drive, then records the outcome and the next due date.
